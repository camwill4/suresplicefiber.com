About

Owner-operated. The splicer you hire is the splicer on site.

Sure Splice Fiber LLC, doing business as CW Splicing, is a Kansas fiber splicing company run by principal splicer Colby Williams. Colby has 27 years of cable splicing behind him, the last 7 in fiber, from single-fiber drops to 864-count ribbon closures.

He splices and tests every job himself and works directly with your project managers and engineers. Nothing passes through layers of supervisors or gets handed to a crew you've never met.

We're based in Leavenworth, Kansas, in the Kansas City metro, so regional jobs carry no per diem. For scoped work we travel anywhere in the continental US, bringing our own trucks, splicers and test gear.

Services

What we do

Splicing and testing come from the same crew, so every fiber we hand over is spliced, tested and documented.

Fusion splicing

Single-fiber and ribbon (mass fusion) splicing, proven to 864 fibers per closure.

Testing & documentation

Bi-directional OTDR, insertion loss and power meter testing. Native .sor traces and PDF reports for every fiber.

Solar, BESS & substations

SCADA and communications fiber for utility-scale solar, battery storage and substations: inverter blocks, collector runs, patch panels.

Data centers

High-count ribbon splicing for backbone and inter-building fiber.

Outside & inside plant

Aerial work from our own bucket van, plus underground, vaults, manholes, handholes and telecom central offices.

Troubleshooting & restoration

OTDR and visual fault location to find breaks, bends and bad splices, then repair them. Overnight cutovers when needed.

Priced per splice, per termination or hourly, to fit your contract.

Equipment & testing

Company-owned gear. Documented results.

Equipment

  • Fusion splicers: Fujikura 60S (single-fiber), Fujikura 60R and Fitel S123M12 (12-fiber ribbon)
  • OTDRs: EXFO MaxTester MAX-715B-M1 and EXFO FTB-1, calibrated 2026
  • Loss testing: EXFO light source, Wilcom and ORIENTek power meters, VeEX PON power meter, visual fault locator
  • Field setup: bucket van, work truck, and an in-van splicing station with a generator for any weather and sites without power
Our working target is 0.10 dB or less per fusion splice, bi-directional average. Any splice over target is re-made before handover.

What every job hands over

  • Splice and fiber assignment records
  • Closeout photos of closures, trays and panels
  • Delivered within 5 business days of finishing, or on your contract's schedule
Safety & insurance

Safe, insured, ready to onboard.
